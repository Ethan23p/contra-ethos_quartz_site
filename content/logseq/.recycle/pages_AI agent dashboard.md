		- A sandbox I've engineered for rapidly experimenting with agentic workflows.

		- Consumer-facing AI products like ChatGPT, Zapier are useful, but I kept reaching for code-based solutions. Writing Python gave me reliability, consistency, access to the full power of agentic AI, and without sacrificing the polished workflows of the consumer apps.
	- ## Solution
		- The natural next step for me was to create custom tools and workflows with the power of agentic AI, the reliability of Python, and the polished user experience of consumer-facing applications.
	- ## Outcome
		- This unlocked significant growth and efficiency in my own work, and revealed an opportunity to help businesses of all sizes achieve the same.
	- ## Insight
		- By automating tedious procedures in existing workplaces, I can grant others more time for what matters most: the human element of work that gets us out of bed in the morning.
		- By turning outdated systems into intuitive, seamless workflows I can help businesses reclaim resources so they can *choose* where to to direct them.
